Frequently Asked Questions

What are the diagnostics charges for damaged UPS or inverters?
We offer fully transparent testing at our Jampur diagnostics laboratory. Standard checking and troubleshooting diagnostics are entirely free of cost if you choose to repair the system through us. Otherwise, testing only tickets start at Rs. 500-1000 depending on board complexity.
Can I buy components and spare parts at wholesale rates?
Yes. Ayan Electronics is the primary wholesale supplier for electronics spares, original power MOSFETs (such as IRF3205, IRFB4110), high-frequency transformers, diagnostic cooling fans, and logic boards in Jampur city and surrounding districts.
Do you offer physical warranty cards on repaired inverters?
Yes, we stand behind our workmanship. Every complete repair service (motherboard swap, Mosfet kit overhaul, or transformer rewinding) comes with a dedicated 1-Month to 3-Month shop warranty depending on components selected.
How does the WhatsApp checkout cart process work?
Add any system model to the cart and fill out your address fields. Clicking Checkout processes the array, formatting your order text. It opens WhatsApp immediately to submit the order directly to Muhammad Yameen Leshari at 03333301123 for price verification, stock lock, and dispatch details.
Do you carry batteries and hybrid solar panels?
Absolutely. We carry Osaka/AGS/Osaka Tubular Batteries, long-life lithium battery configurations, and Mono PERC Tier-1 solar panels with full manufacturer warranty.
Where is Ayan Electronics physically located?
We are located in the center of Jampur City at Almasoom Street, City Plaza, Traffic Chowk, Jampur.
